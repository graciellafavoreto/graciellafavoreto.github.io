Research

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision and medical imaging. My current work investigates deep learning approaches for diagnostic support, including segmentation and classification of pulmonary conditions, multimodal analysis involving X-ray and CT, and methods for making model behavior more interpretable and explainable.
